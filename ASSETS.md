# Artwork and fonts

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers the project's code, original documentation,
favicon, and project-created artwork to the extent rights in that artwork are
held by the contributors.

## Artwork

- `dist/assets/antique-board.{png,webp}`
- `dist/assets/cowrie-shell.{png,webp}`
- `dist/assets/tulunadu-family.{png,webp}`
- `dist/favicon.svg`

The board, shell, and family images were created with AI assistance. The board is
an artistic interpretation of antique wood, not an authenticated reconstruction.
The family scene depicts imagined people. Reference photographs are not included.

## Fonts

The page requests DM Sans and Libre Caslon Display through Google Fonts. Font files
are not bundled; system fonts provide a fallback. If you self-host the fonts,
include the licenses supplied with those files.
